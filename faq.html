Prices and paying

Is the price fixed?

Yes. You see one fixed price for the whole car before you book, not a price per person, and once your booking is confirmed it doesn’t change unless you change your journey or we wait longer than the free waiting time. There’s never any surge pricing. Our fixed airport prices are the same day and night, with the drop-off and car park fees already in, and so are MPV journeys within Southampton. Other journeys cost more late at night, all journeys cost more over Christmas and New Year, and the booking form always shows the price for your date and time.

Are airport fees and the London Congestion Charge included?

Yes. Airport drop-off fees, and the car park fee while we wait for you at an airport, are charged at cost and already in your price, including stops at an airport on the way. Coming back from an airport usually costs a little more than going, because the car park fee is more than the drop-off fee. Journeys into central London’s Congestion Charge zone during charging hours include the £18 daily charge.

How and when do I pay?

Pay by cash, card or bank transfer. You can also book and pay by card online, and you’ll get an email confirmation and receipt straight away. If we’re collecting you from an airport or from outside Southampton, please pay in advance: a last-minute cancellation or no-show would otherwise leave us paying for a wasted journey and the car park. When we confirm your booking, we’ll send you a secure card payment link, or our bank details if you’d rather pay by transfer.

Payment links and bank details only ever come from our WhatsApp, 07541 109738, or ridecresttaxis@gmail.com, and the account name is always RideCrest Taxis Limited, our registered company name. Please use your booking reference as the payment reference. If anyone asks you to pay another way or into a different account, call us on 07541 109738 before you pay.

How are private tours priced?

You pay for the driving, plus the time you spend at each place: £30 an hour, or £40 in an Executive. You choose the places and how long to stay at each one when you book, and see your price and a timetable for the day straight away. After the first 3 hours at your stops, each extra hour is half price, so longer days are better value. We allow extra driving time for traffic, and running up to 30 minutes late is fine. If you’d like longer on the day, extra time is charged at the same rates.

Booking and changes

When is my booking confirmed?

Once we reply to confirm it, or straight away when you book and pay online. Sending your booking by WhatsApp or email doesn’t book the car on its own, so if you haven’t heard from us, please get in touch. We reply to messages 7am–10pm Sunday to Thursday, and 7am–2am on Friday and Saturday nights, so anything sent overnight is answered first thing. For a car within the next couple of hours, call 07541 109738 rather than booking online.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Just message or call us. For airport, cruise and private tour journeys, and journeys to or from anywhere outside Southampton, cancelling is free more than 48 hours before pickup, half the price between 48 and 24 hours before, and the full price within 24 hours. For journeys within Southampton, cancelling is free up to 2 hours before pickup, half the price after that, and the full price once your driver is on the way. If you’re not at the pickup point when the free waiting time ends and we can’t reach you, the driver may leave and the full price is charged. There’s no charge if we cancel or can’t get a car to you on time.

If you’ve paid in advance, we refund what you paid minus any cancellation charge, to the card or account you paid from, and in full if we can’t take your booking. Card refunds usually show within 5 to 10 working days. If the car needs cleaning after a journey (after sickness, for example), there’s a charge of up to £100. If anything isn’t right, tell us at ridecresttaxis@gmail.com or on 07541 109738 and we’ll put it right.

How far do you go?

Anywhere in the UK. Most of our journeys start or end in Southampton: all UK airports, the cruise terminals in Southampton and elsewhere, day tours, and long-distance door-to-door trips. The booking form prices any UK journey.

On the day

How long will my driver wait?

Airports: we track your flight, so delays don’t count. Your driver waits up to 75 minutes after you land, to allow for passport control and baggage, and the car park fee for that time is already in your price. Cruises: 30 minutes free from your booked time, so choose the time you expect to be off the ship with your luggage. Anywhere else: 15 minutes free. After that, waiting is 40p a minute, plus any extra car park fee at cost.

How will I find my driver at the airport?

We meet you in the arrivals hall holding a name board. We track your flight, so we know when you land even if it’s early or late. If you can’t see us, call or WhatsApp 07541 109738.

Which Southampton cruise terminal is my ship at?

Your cruise line’s documents or app will say. Southampton has five terminals:

  • Ocean (berth 46/47) and QEII (berth 38/39), both through Dock Gate 4
  • City (berth 101), Horizon (berth 102) and Mayflower (berth 106), all through Dock Gate 10

Cruise lines sometimes change terminals close to sailing, so add your ship’s name when you book and we’ll check where it is on the day.

Your journey

How much luggage fits in each car?
  • Saloon: up to 4 passengers and 2 large suitcases
  • Estate: up to 4 passengers and 3 large suitcases
  • Executive: up to 3 passengers and 2 large suitcases
  • MPV 6-seater: up to 6 passengers and 4 large suitcases
  • MPV 8-seater: up to 8 passengers and 8 large suitcases

When you book, cars that are too small for your group or luggage are greyed out. Cruise holidays often mean extra luggage, so if you’re close to the limit, choose the next size up. Tell us about golf clubs, bikes, pushchairs or other bulky items in the note.

Do you provide child seats?

No, but you’re welcome to bring your own.

Can you help with access needs or assistance dogs?

Yes. Assistance dogs travel free, and we never charge more because of a disability or for carrying a wheelchair or mobility aid. Tell us what help you need when you book. Other pets by arrangement.

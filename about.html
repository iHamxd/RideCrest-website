About RideCrest

A small private hire firm based in Southampton. We drive pre-booked airport and cruise transfers, private day tours, and door-to-door journeys around Southampton and across the UK.

How we work

  • Booked in advance, priced up front. You see one fixed price for the whole car before you book, and there’s never any surge pricing.
  • Licensed in Southampton. Southampton City Council licenses RideCrest as a private hire operator, and licenses our drivers and cars. Every driver is DBS-checked.
  • On time for flights and ships. We track your flight, meet you in arrivals with a name board, and wait up to 75 minutes after you land. At the cruise terminals, the first 30 minutes of waiting are free.
  • Help when you need it. Assistance dogs travel free, and we never charge more because of a disability or for carrying a wheelchair or mobility aid.

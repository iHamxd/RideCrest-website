            Good to know

            Quick answers before you book.

            See all questions

            Is the price fixed?

            Yes. You see one fixed price for the whole car before you book, and there’s never any surge pricing. It only changes if you change your journey, or we wait longer than the free waiting time.

            What if my flight is delayed?

            We track your flight, so delays don’t count. Your driver waits up to 75 minutes after you land, to allow for passport control and baggage, and the car park fee for that time is already in your price.

            How do I pay?

            By cash, card or bank transfer. You can also book and pay by card online. If we’re collecting you from an airport or from outside Southampton, please pay in advance. We’ll send a secure payment link when we confirm your booking.

            Can I change or cancel?

            Yes, just message or call us. Airport, cruise and tour journeys, and journeys to or from outside Southampton, are free to cancel more than 48 hours before pickup. Journeys within Southampton are free to cancel up to 2 hours before. Cancellation terms

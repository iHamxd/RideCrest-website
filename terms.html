Booking terms

These terms apply to every journey you book with RideCrest. If anything isn’t clear, call or WhatsApp 07541 109738.

Last updated October 2026

Our terms

Who we are

RideCrest is a trading name of RideCrest Taxis Limited (company no. 17420363), a private hire operator licensed by Southampton City Council (operator licence OP 428). Your contract is with RideCrest, whichever of our drivers and cars carries out your journey.

Booking

Every journey is booked in advance, as our cars can’t be stopped in the street. Your booking is confirmed when we reply with your booking reference, or straight away when you book and pay online. Please check it and tell us straight away if anything is wrong.

Prices

You agree one fixed price for the whole car before you travel. It only changes if you change your journey, add stops, or we wait longer than the free waiting time. Airport drop-off and car park fees are already included. Journeys into central London’s Congestion Charge zone during charging hours include the £18 charge. Journeys cost more late at night, except our fixed airport prices and MPV journeys within Southampton, and all journeys cost more over Christmas and New Year.

Waiting

Airports: we track your flight and wait up to 75 minutes after you land. Cruises: 30 minutes free from your booked time. Anywhere else: 15 minutes free. After that, waiting is 40p a minute, plus any extra car park fee at cost.

Paying

Pay by cash, card or bank transfer. Pickups from any airport, or from outside Southampton, must be paid in advance. Use your booking reference as the payment reference. We never change our bank details by message: if anyone asks you to pay a different account, call 07541 109738 before you pay.

Cancelling and no-shows

For airport, cruise and private tour journeys, and journeys to or from anywhere outside Southampton, cancelling is free more than 48 hours before pickup, half the price between 48 and 24 hours before, and the full price within 24 hours. For journeys within Southampton, cancelling is free up to 2 hours before pickup, half the price after that, and the full price once your driver is on the way. If you’re not at the pickup when the free waiting time ends and we can’t reach you, the driver may leave and the full price is charged. There’s no charge if we cancel or can’t get a car to you on time.

If you’ve paid in advance, we refund what you paid minus any cancellation charge, to the card or account you paid from, and in full if we cancel or can’t take your booking. Card refunds usually show within 5 to 10 working days.

Changes

Tell us about any change as soon as you can, and we’ll confirm any change in price first. If we need to send a different or larger car, we’ll only do so with your agreement.

In the car

Everyone wears a seatbelt, and there’s no smoking or vaping. We can’t carry more passengers than the car is licensed for, or luggage that won’t fit safely. We don’t provide child seats, but you’re welcome to bring your own. Assistance dogs travel free, and we never charge more because of a disability. If the car needs cleaning after your journey, for example after sickness, there’s a charge of up to £100.

Delays

We plan every journey with time for normal traffic, so please tell us your check-in or boarding time. We’re not responsible for delays outside our control, such as accidents, road closures or severe weather. Nothing in these terms limits our responsibility for death or injury caused by our negligence, or your other legal rights.

Lost property

Tell us as soon as you can. We keep items safe for 28 days and help you get them back. Any delivery cost is yours.

If we can’t do your journey

We may pass your booking to another licensed private hire operator, and we’ll tell you who before your journey. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.

Complaints

Call or WhatsApp 07541 109738, or email ridecresttaxis@gmail.com, with your booking reference or the date and time of your journey, and tell us what happened. If you ever feel unsafe, call 999 first.

We confirm we’ve received your complaint within 2 working days, and tell you what we found and what we’ll do within 14 days. We record every complaint, and report complaints about a driver’s behaviour or safety to Southampton City Council’s licensing team within three working days.

If you’re still unhappy, our director will look at it again. You can also contact Southampton City Council’s licensing team, which licenses our drivers, our cars and us.
